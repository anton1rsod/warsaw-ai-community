## Why CC0

This dedication aligns with **ADR-0001** (OSS-first / MIT licensing posture). The Subploters brand and the community it identifies are open-by-default. Any Subploter — and anyone outside the community — is free to use these assets without administrative overhead. Closed-brand legal posture would contradict the community's open-source-spirit value (see `brand.md` §6).
