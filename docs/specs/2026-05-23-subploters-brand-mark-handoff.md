**Pattern across rejections:**
- Claude/Anthropic-adjacent radial sparkle = rejected from round 1
- Cross-shaped marks (dagger, reference mark) = rejected as religious/medical
- Name-rooted experiments (S monogram, underline, subscript dot, parens, diagrams) = rejected as off-feel
- Gem direction was approved as concept but no specific cut landed
- Final two refined brilliants also rejected

**What this might mean for chat-37:** continuing to iterate glyph variations is unlikely to land. After ~7 rounds the founder is fatigued and the problem may not be glyph-shape — it may be a different problem entirely.

## ★ Chat-37 owns

Two viable paths. The next chat decides which:

### Path A: Hire a designer

- The Subploters brand is past the "iterate until something lands" stage. Bring a professional brand designer (freelance or boutique) with the locked spec in `community/brand/brand.md` + the tagline + the visual identity + the multi-location architecture as the brief.
- Budget consideration: 2,000–8,000 EUR for a professional mark from a competent indie designer. Worth it for a brand that needs to scale to multiple cities and a venture studio over years.
- The locked spec already gives a designer 80% of what they need. Only the mark needs creative work.

### Path B: Ship typographic-only

- Skip the separate mark entirely. The brand is the **wordmark** `Subploters` in Geist 600 + amber + cream + the city stamp `PSA · CITY`. That's already a complete brand system.
- Many successful brands have no separate symbol mark — *Linear*, *Stripe*, *Notion*, *Vercel* originally, *The Browser Company*. They ship wordmark-only.
- The "asterisk slot" can be left empty for now — `Subploters` (no mark) is a valid wordmark.
- Unblock favicon (use typographic `S` or first-letter monogram in Geist 700 amber on cream), OG image, platform wiring.
- Revisit a custom symbol mark later when there's a designer involved.

### Path C (don't recommend, but possible): Continue iterating

- Try a wholly new direction not yet explored — e.g., custom illustrated mark from a brand designer, photographic / textural treatment, animated mark, abstract painterly form.
- Risk: another round of rejections costs another session of fatigue.
