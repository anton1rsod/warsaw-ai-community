Findings

Unanimous (6/6)

  • The real incumbent is free Telegram, not Meetup. The wedge vs. paid tools is real; the wedge vs. a free habit is UX only.
  • Pain is documented, but never quoted. 33 fragmented channels, 37k followers on a diaspora events feed — yet zero verbatim organizer-pain quotes survived honest search.
  • Free-first is the flaw — and it's fixable. Every PIVOT lands on monetization timing / price model. Couchsurfing shows monetize-later is also trust-risky later.
  • Distribution is the strongest pillar. First 100 users ≈ one cross-post; first 20 organizers ≈ a DM list.
  • Drop the Locals.md claim. It failed verification (the platform appears alive) — flagged independently by three personas.

Where the panel splits

  • Viability 1 vs 2 — a clean lens split. Commercial seats (Anton, Mark, Nazar) refuse to score above 1 without a single invoice; engineering seats (Dmitriy, Heorhii, Maksym) credit the comparables with a 2.
  • Market 2 vs 3. Maksym credits comparable density; the rest hold at 2 on the bottom-up math.

Timing tailwinds

  • Meetup price shock (2024→) — a live migration list of angry organizers.
  • FB Community Chats killed Oct 2025; organic reach 1–2%.
  • IRL-social wave: Timeleft €18M ARR; Joiner €500k into Poland.

The experiment — the panel converged on one move: charge organizers now

STEP 1 · 7 DAYS · €0

WTP DM test

DM 10–20 named Warsaw organizers. One sentence: "Kolo replaces Telegram signup chaos — €15/mo, cancel anytime." Invoice today, not a waitlist.

PASS: ≥3 paid commitments (card on file)
STEP 2 · 30 DAYS

Paid-ticket pilot

2–3 organizers already selling tickets route one event each through Kolo on Stripe Connect at 5–10% commission. Agent model — no PSD2 license needed; VAT on commission only.

PASS: ≥3 transactions cleared + organizer asks to repeat
IN PARALLEL

Mom-Test interviews

10–15 organizer interviews to capture the missing verbatim pain. Past behavior and current spend only — no "would you use it".

PASS: ≥7/10 name signup admin in top-3 pains unprompted
